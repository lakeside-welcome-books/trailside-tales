Where the Trail Meets the Lake

Harrison trailhead (mile 15.3) south about 7 to 7.5 miles to the Chatcolet Bridge and Heyburn State Park. Cycling, one way.

Written by Dave, a Harrison local. Narrated by a computer voice (Chatterbox).

?How this draft works

This is a simple web tour. On each stop, tap Play when you reach that landmark. Your phone will not start the stories by itself (GPS auto-play may come later).

  1. Start at the Harrison trailhead by the public beach. Play the Intro before you roll out.
  2. Ride south along the lake. At each landmark below, open that stop and tap Play.
  3. Keep volume low (or one earbud) so you can hear bells and voices around you.
  4. Stay on the pavement and at rest stops. Carry your own drinking water.
Play clean. The old rail bed was built with mine waste. The trail’s asphalt is a clean cap. Stay on the trail, wash hands before eating, and brush dirt off shoes, gear, and pets. Advice from Idaho Parks & Recreation and the Coeur d’Alene Basin cleanup program.
Still a draft. Some stops are waiting on review from the Crane Historical Society and the Coeur d’Alene Tribe’s cultural staff. Those stops are marked below. Do not treat this page as a final published tour.
